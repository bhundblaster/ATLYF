Before you start

Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before their first sachet.

Psyllium is one of the most-studied soluble fibers and is widely used as a daily supplement. Start with one sachet a day, drink it with a full glass of water, and give your gut a week or two to settle in. If you're pregnant, nursing, or managing a medical condition, check with your doctor first.

One sachet into a full glass — around 250 to 300ml of cold water. Stir once and drink it while it's still fizzing. Psyllium keeps thickening as it sits, so it's best in the first minute, not the fifth.

That was the entire brief. Each flavor was built as a drink first and a fiber supplement second — no chalk, no grit, and no aftertaste you need to chase away with something else. Masala Cola in particular tends to surprise people.

Fiber can slow how quickly some medicines are absorbed. The usual guidance is to leave about a two-hour gap either side of your medication — and to run it past your doctor if you're on anything long-term.

Each flavor is built around natural, non-sugar sweeteners, so you get the taste without the added sugar — the same 5g of fiber either way.
